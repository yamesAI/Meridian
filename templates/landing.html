What is a Saturn Return?

Saturn takes 29.5 years to orbit the sun. When it returns to where it was at your birth, you enter a 2-3 year window of intense restructuring — especially in career, identity, and financial structures. Traditional astrologers considered it the single most important transit of a person's first 30 years.

Guido Bonatti dedicated an entire section of the *Liber Astronomiae* to what he called *electional astrology* — the art of choosing the right moment to begin an action. His criteria for business launches were specific, technical, and tested over decades of practice.

The core principle: the chart of the moment you begin something becomes the natal chart of that thing. A business launched at a favorable moment carries favorable signatures. A business launched at a void of course Moon, or with the ruler of the 10th house in detriment, begins its life weakened.

## The Traditional Rules for Business Launch Timing

**1. Avoid the Void of Course Moon**

The Moon is void of course when it has made its last major aspect to any planet before leaving its current sign. Traditional astrologers agreed: actions begun during a void Moon "come to nothing." This doesn't mean catastrophe — it means the venture doesn't develop as intended, drifts, or loses momentum without any specific cause.

Knowing the void Moon windows for the next two weeks and avoiding them costs nothing. Launching in a void Moon and wondering why nothing takes off costs significantly more.

**2. Ascendant and 10th House Lord in Good Condition**

The Ascendant of the launch chart represents the venture itself. The 10th house represents its public reputation and career/commerce topics. The planets ruling these houses should be dignified, direct, and well-aspected.

A launch with the 10th house ruler in retrograde often produces a venture that struggles to gain public traction regardless of the quality of the product.

**3. Jupiter or Venus Strongly Placed**

The traditional benefics — Jupiter (greater benefic) and Venus (lesser benefic) — should ideally be angular (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th houses) and dignified. This signals that the benefic influence is concentrated where it matters.

**4. Saturn Not Afflicting the Ascendant or 10th**

An applying Saturn square or opposition to the Ascendant of the launch chart suggests significant early delays and structural obstacles. This isn't fatal, but it's a headwind that could be avoided with 2-3 days of flexibility in the launch date.

**5. Mercury Direct (for communication-based businesses)**

For any venture that involves writing, communication, contracts, negotiations, or online presence — which in 2026 is nearly every business — Mercury should be direct and ideally dignified. Mercury retrograde launches often produce contractual confusion, miscommunication, and technology problems in the first 3-6 months.

## Using Your Personal Transits to Confirm

The electional chart alone isn't sufficient. It needs to align with your personal transits. A perfect electional chart launched during a period when your natal Saturn is being hammered by transiting Mars still carries that natal pressure into the venture.

The ideal timing is when: the electional chart is favorable AND your current transits support action in the relevant life area AND your profection year's time lord is well-aspected.
